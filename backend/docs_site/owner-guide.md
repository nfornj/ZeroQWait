# Registering Your Shop

ZeroQwait gives every shop owner a personal AI operations team. Once you register, you get a **Receptionist**, **Finance**, and **HR** AI agent — all accessible from a single chat inbox.

---

## Register via the AI Agent

The fastest way to get started is through the AI chat on [zeroqwait.com](https://zeroqwait.com).

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">1</div>
  <div class="step-body">
    Open the chat agent at <a href="https://zeroqwait.com">zeroqwait.com</a> and type:
    <br><br>
    <span class="chip">I want to register my shop</span>
    <br>or<br>
    <span class="chip">Register a shop</span>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">2</div>
  <div class="step-body">
    The agent will guide you through the registration form step by step, asking for:
    <ul>
      <li>Shop name</li>
      <li>Business category (Barber, Salon, Clinic, Auto Shop, etc.)</li>
      <li>City / location</li>
      <li>Your email address</li>
      <li>A password for your owner account</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-num">3</div>
  <div class="step-body">
    Once registered, you'll receive your <strong>shop's unique URL</strong>:
    <br><code>https://&#60;your-shop-slug&#62;.zeroqwait.com</code>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="tip">

**Your shop is live immediately.** Share the URL with customers so they can start joining your queue.

</div>

## Owner Dashboard

After logging in at your shop URL, you have access to:

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Queue** | See who's in queue, call next, close/open queue |
| **Analytics** | Revenue charts, peak hours, service popularity |
| **Employees** | Add/remove staff, assign shifts |
| **Services** | Add services, set prices and durations |
| **Settings** | Hours, holidays, shop profile |
| **AI Inbox** | Chat with your AI agent team |

---

## AI Agent Inbox

The **Agent Inbox** is your command centre. You can manage your entire shop by chatting in plain English with your AI team.

**Example commands:**

<span class="chip">What was yesterday's revenue?</span>  
<span class="chip">How many customers did we serve this week?</span>  
<span class="chip">Add a new employee called Maria</span>  
<span class="chip">Show me today's shift schedule</span>  
<span class="chip">Close the queue for today</span>  
<span class="chip">What's our most popular service this month?</span>

The Supervisor AI routes your message to the right specialist:
- **Queue / bookings** → Receptionist agent
- **Revenue / analytics** → Finance agent
- **Staff / schedules** → HR agent

---

## Managing Queues

From the dashboard queue panel:

- **Call Next** — marks the current customer as served and advances the queue
- **Close Queue** — stops new customers from joining (requires your approval via the AI Inbox)
- **Open Queue** — re-opens for walk-ins

Customers see live queue updates on their end automatically.
