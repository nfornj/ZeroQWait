# ⚡ ZeroQwait Platform

**ZeroQwait** is an AI-powered queue management platform that connects customers with service businesses — barbers, salons, clinics, auto shops, and more — through real-time, AI-assisted queues.

No apps to download. No account required for customers. Just open the website, find a shop, and join the queue.

## How It Works

When you land on [zeroqwait.com](https://zeroqwait.com), you'll see the **ZeroQwait hero section** with a large, glowing **violet orb** floating at the centre of the page. This is the **Master AI Agent** entry point — your starting point for everything.

The orb slowly floats up and down with a purple glow animation. Hover over it to reveal the **"MEET ZEROQ"** label, then **click it** to open the full-screen AI chat overlay.

The agent understands natural language and can help you with three things:

<div class="tip">

**The three things ZeroQwait can do for you:**

1. 🏪 **Register a Shop** — Set up your business and get your own AI agent team
2. 🔍 **Search for Shops** — Find services nearby and join an AI-powered queue
3. 💬 **Ask about our Products** — Learn about pricing, features, and how it works

</div>

Simply open the chat and describe what you need in plain English. There's no need to navigate menus or fill out forms — the agent will guide you through everything step by step.

## Platform Overview

```
You
 │
 ▼
zeroqwait.com  ──→  AI Chat Agent  ──→  Find a Shop  ──→  Join Queue  ──→  Get Served
                                   └──→  Register Shop ──→  AI Operations Team
```

- **Customers** interact with a shop's AI Receptionist agent to discover services, get wait time estimates, and join queues — all via chat or voice.
- **Shop owners** manage their entire business through natural-language chat with an AI team: Receptionist, Finance, and HR agents.
- **Employees** receive shift assignments and manage individual queues through their dashboard.
